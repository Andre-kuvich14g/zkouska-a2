Czech A2 exam for permanent residence: frequently asked questions

A short guide to the new exam format (from 11 April 2026), points, price, registration and results. Checked against the official website cestina-pro-cizince.cz (November 2026).

What is the A2 exam for permanent residence (trvalý pobyt)?
It is a Czech language exam at level A2. You submit the certificate together with your application for permanent residence in the Czech Republic. Since 11 April 2026 the exam has a new format.
What parts does the exam have?
  • Čtení (reading) — 40 minutes, 25 points
  • Psaní (writing) — 25 minutes, 20 points
  • Poslech (listening) — about 40 minutes, 25 points
  • Mluvení (speaking) — 15 minutes, 40 points
Everything takes place on one day: the written part lasts almost 2 hours, the oral part about 15 minutes.
How many points do I need to pass?
Written part (Čtení + Psaní + Poslech): at least 42 of 70 points (60 %). Oral part (Mluvení): at least 24 of 40 (60 %). If you fail even one part, you have to retake the whole exam.
How much does the exam cost?
3,200 CZK. The school must receive the payment no later than 8 days before the exam.
How do I register?
Through the online application on the official website cestina-pro-cizince.cz or in person at one of the exam schools. The school will tell you when a place is available. You have to check and sign the application in person at the school, so your data must be accurate.
When and where is the exam held?
At accredited schools all over the Czech Republic (list at cestina-pro-cizince.cz), usually on Wednesday or Saturday from 9:00. The number of places and dates is gradually increasing.
What should I bring?
A valid passport (cestovní doklad). The school does not accept other documents.
When will I get the result and the certificate?
The result comes by e-mail or SMS within 9 days. You can collect the certificate (Osvědčení o znalosti českého jazyka) in person within 30 days after the exam or receive it by post — as chosen in your application. The certificate is valid indefinitely.
Can I cancel or move the exam?
At least 15 days before — e-mail the school with your full name, date of birth, passport number (and registration code, if you have one). Later you can cancel only because of illness or another serious reason with a document.
How long should I prepare?
Officially at least 50 hours of a course or self-study are recommended. On our website you can take 20 mock exams in the new format, study Speaking and Writing lessons with audio and watch a new video lesson on YouTube every day.
Does everyone have to take this exam?
No. Some people are exempt (for example because of age or health) — the exact rules are in Nařízení vlády č. 31/2016 Sb. If in doubt, call the Ministry of the Interior info line: +420 974 801 801 (Mon–Thu 8–16, Fri 8–12).
Who made this website?
“Zkouška A2 nanečisto” is an unofficial free learning project made by Andre, a Ukrainian living in the Czech Republic. No ads, no registration. The website is not connected with the exam organisers, so always check the current rules on the official website before registering.
